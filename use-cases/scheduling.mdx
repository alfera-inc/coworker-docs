Scheduling should not require a human sitting between a calendar and an inbox. Alfera connects to Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, and Slack so you can automate confirmations, reminders, and rescheduling with a single instruction.

## Confirm and Remind

No-shows waste time and revenue. Alfera makes sure every appointment is confirmed and remembered.

- "Send a confirmation email to everyone who booked a call today via Calendly."
- "Text a reminder 2 hours before every client meeting and include the Zoom link."
- "Post tomorrow's appointment list to the #operations Slack channel every evening at 6 PM."

Alfera checks calendar entries, reads booking sources, and sends reminders in your company tone. Because anything customer-facing waits for one-tap approval, you can review and edit before it goes out.

<Note>
  Alfera learns your reminder cadence and preferred channels over time, so confirmations feel natural and consistent.
</Note>

## Reschedule Cleanly

Last-minute changes are inevitable. Alfera handles the back-and-forth for you.

- "Find any meeting where the guest replied 'can we move this' and propose 3 alternate slots from my calendar."
- "Reschedule all recurring team standups next week because of the holiday and notify every attendee."
- "Move any appointment marked 'canceled' in Calendly to a 'to-be-rescheduled' list and ping the sales rep."

It reads replies, checks live availability, updates invites, and logs every change so no one is left guessing.

## Keep the Team in Sync

A shared schedule only works if it is accurate. Alfera keeps calendars and trackers aligned.

- "Block focus time on every engineer's calendar when Jira shows them assigned to a sprint task."
- "Sync out-of-office requests from the HR system to the team calendar and update Slack status."
- "Compare the staffing roster in Google Sheets to the shift calendar and flag uncovered slots."

Alfera reads from multiple sources and writes updates back so your team schedule stays the single source of truth.

<Tip>
  Trigger scheduling tasks from events (new booking, cancellation reply, calendar conflict) so Alfera reacts in real time instead of you checking manually.
</Tip>
