Alfera lives in your support tools so you do not have to. Connect Zendesk, HubSpot, Intercom, Gmail, or Slack, describe the job in plain English, and Alfera will read tickets, draft replies, confirm appointments, and escalate only the exceptions that truly need a person.

## Triage and Answer

Start every morning with a clean queue instead of a backlog.

Ask Alfera to scan your open tickets and do the repetitive work first:

- "Reply to all open Zendesk tickets under 24 hours old with a status update."
- "Draft responses to every HubSpot conversation where the last message is from the customer and it is a how-to question."
- "Send a holding reply to anyone who has been waiting more than 4 hours."

Alfera reads the ticket history, checks your connected knowledge base or Google Docs, and writes a reply in your company tone. Anything customer-facing waits for your one-tap approval before it sends, so you stay in control.

<Note>
  Alfera learns your tone, standard phrases, and policies over time, so drafts get sharper with every run.
</Note>

## Confirm and Schedule

Appointments and bookings are a common source of back-and-forth. Alfera can handle that loop for you.

- "Confirm tomorrow's calls from Calendly and send reminder emails 2 hours before."
- "Find every ticket that mentions 'demo request' and book a time that works for the assignee."
- "Reschedule any meeting where the contact replied they are unavailable."

Alfera checks calendars, sends confirmations, and updates the ticket or CRM record so your team always knows what is happening.

## Summarize and Escalate

Not every ticket can be solved automatically. Alfera surfaces the ones that matter.

- "Summarize all open P1 tickets and post the list to the #support-escalations Slack channel every morning at 9 AM."
- "Flag any ticket where the customer mentioned 'refund,' 'legal,' or 'cancel account' and assign it to the senior queue."
- "Generate a weekly report of top support themes and send it to the leadership team."

You get a plain-English audit trail for every action Alfera took: what it read, what it changed, what it sent, and what it skipped.

<Tip>
  Set Alfera to run on a schedule (every morning, every hour, or after specific triggers like a new ticket) so your support queue never silently fills up again.
</Tip>
