---

You can get Alfera working for you in under five minutes. Sign up, connect a tool, describe a task in plain English, and watch Alfera plan and execute it across your apps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for free">
    Go to [app.alfera.ai](https://app.alfera.ai) and create your account. It is free to start, and you do not need a credit card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your first tool">
    Choose a tool you already use, such as Gmail or HubSpot. Alfera connects with one-click OAuth, so you do not have to copy and paste API keys or server URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your first task">
    Send Alfera a message in Slack (or mention it in Teams or Gmail) in plain English. For example: "Follow up on all leads who haven't replied in 3 days." Alfera will plan the steps, read what it needs, and start working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">
    Alfera will show you a plain-English summary of everything it read, changed, or sent. If a step is customer-facing or destructive, it waits for your one-tap approval before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it to run automatically">
    Choose whether this task should run on a schedule or be triggered by an event, such as a new lead or Monday morning. Alfera will keep working in the background and report back with results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Looking for inspiration? Browse the [Use Cases](/use-cases/customer-support) section to see how teams use Alfera for support, sales, operations, bookkeeping, scheduling, and ecommerce.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Approval gates are built in by default. Anything customer-facing or destructive pauses for your review, so you stay in control even when tasks run automatically.
</Note>
