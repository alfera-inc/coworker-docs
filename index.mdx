## What Alfera does for your team

Alfera works across every department in your business. Here is what it handles out of the box:

- **Support** — Triage incoming tickets, draft replies, escalate issues, and update your help desk automatically.
- **Sales** — Follow up with leads, update CRM records, book meetings, and send proposals without manual work.
- **Operations** — Run daily checklists, coordinate between teams, and keep processes moving with zero supervision.
- **Finance** — Chase overdue invoices, reconcile transactions, generate reports, and sync data with QuickBooks or Stripe.
- **Marketing** — Pull campaign data, compile reports, and schedule outreach based on live metrics.
- **HR** — Onboard employees, collect documents, and answer routine internal questions so your team stays focused.
