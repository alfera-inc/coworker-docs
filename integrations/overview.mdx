Alfera connects to more than 3,000 tools so it can act across your entire stack. You describe tasks in plain English, and Alfera plans and executes the work across any tools it has access to. Here is how those integrations are organized and what they unlock.

## Office Layer

These are the tools your team already lives in every day. Alfera reads, writes, and sends on your behalf so nothing falls through the cracks.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar. Alfera books, reschedules, and cancels meetings.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Spreadsheets" icon="table">
    Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel Online, and more. Alfera reads data, fills rows, and builds reports.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat" icon="message-circle">
    Slack and Microsoft Teams. This is where you describe tasks and receive updates.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Documents" icon="file-text">
    Google Docs, Microsoft Word Online, Notion, and Confluence. Alfera drafts memos, SOPs, and documentation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Presentations" icon="presentation">
    Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint. Alfera builds pitch decks and status updates.
  </Card>

  <Card title="File Storage" icon="folder">
    Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, and Box. Alfera fetches files, uploads deliverables, and organizes folders.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
