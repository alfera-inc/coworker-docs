Approval gates are Alfera's built-in safety check. Before any action that could affect a customer or alter important data, Alfera pauses and asks you to confirm. You decide with a single tap whether it proceeds.

## How approvals work

By default, Alfera pauses on anything customer-facing or destructive. That includes:

- Sending an email or message to a customer
- Updating a deal stage, contact record, or order
- Processing a refund or payment
- Changing inventory, pricing, or policy settings
- Posting publicly on social channels

When Alfera reaches one of these checkpoints, it sends you a message in Slack (and in the app dashboard) with a clear summary of the proposed action. You tap **Approve** to continue or **Reject** to stop it. Alfera explains what happens next in plain English.

## How to approve or reject

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive the pause notification">
    Alfera sends you a Slack message or app notification when it reaches a gate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Read the plain-English explanation of what Alfera wants to do, why, and which tool it targets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap once">
    Click **Approve** to let it continue, or **Reject** to cancel that specific step and move on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure what requires approval

You can customize which types of actions need your explicit sign-off. Go to **Settings \> Approvals** in the Alfera app and toggle categories on or off.

| Category | Default behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Send to customer | Approval required |
| Update CRM record | Approval required |
| Process payment or refund | Approval required |
| Draft email or message | Runs automatically |
| Read and summarize data | Runs automatically |
| Enrich lead data | Runs automatically |

<Note>
  Routine work, like reading data, drafting internal notes, or enriching a lead profile, runs without interruption so Alfera can stay fast.
</Note>
