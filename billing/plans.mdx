Alfera offers three tiers so you can start small and scale as Alfera takes on more of your work. Every plan includes access to integrations, the dashboard, and Slack connectivity.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | \$50/month | Custom |
| Credits | Starter credits included | 5,000/month (scales to 3,000,000 self-serve) | Custom |
| Scheduled and recurring runs | No | Yes | Yes |
| Team seats and shared workspace | No | Yes | Yes |
| Priority support | No | Yes | Yes |
| Custom data regions and residency | No | No | Yes |
| Self-hosted / on-prem option | No | No | Yes |
| Computer Use with custom config | No | No | Yes |
| Security review / DPA / SAML SSO | No | No | Yes |
| SCIM provisioning and audit logs | No | No | Yes |
| Annual invoicing | No | No | Yes |
| 99.9% uptime SLA | No | No | Yes |
| Dedicated onboarding and solutions engineer | No | No | Yes |

### Free

Start at no cost with no credit card required. Free includes starter credits to run real tasks and experience Alfera end to end. Scheduled and recurring runs are not included.

### Pro

At \$50 per month, Pro includes 5,000 credits and scales up to 3,000,000 credits self-serve. You get all integrations, scheduled and recurring runs, a shared workspace with team seats, and priority support.

### Enterprise

Enterprise adds custom pricing and everything in Pro plus custom data regions and residency, a self-hosted or on-premise option, Computer Use with custom configuration, security review and DPA, SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning and audit logs, annual invoicing, a 99.9% uptime SLA, and a dedicated onboarding specialist and solutions engineer.

<Note>
  No annual contract is required. Cancel anytime from your billing settings.
</Note>
