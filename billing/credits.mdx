Credits are how you pay for the work Alfera does. You buy credits in monthly packages and spend them only when Alfera executes a task. Seats, integrations, and idle time are never charged.

## How credits work

- **1 dollar = 100 credits**
- **Pro plan = 5,000 credits per month** (\$50)
- You are charged only when a task runs. If Alfera is idle, your balance does not decrease.

## Typical credit usage

| Task type | Credits used | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple task | 10-30 credits | Reply to a single customer email |
| Multi-step workflow | 200-600 credits | Triage a batch of leads and update the CRM |
| Long-running job | 5,000\+ credits | Outreach campaign to 500 prospects |

## Keeping control of spend

- **Choose your monthly package** and change it anytime from billing settings
- **Every run has approval gates**, so you review costly actions before they execute
- **Live usage view** in the dashboard shows exactly where your credits go
- **Runs pause with a clear message if you hit zero** — nothing is charged beyond your balance

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="What do credits pay for?">
  Credits cover the compute, AI model usage, and tool actions Alfera consumes while running your tasks. You are never charged for seats, connecting integrations, or idle time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I run out?">
  If your credit balance reaches zero, Alfera pauses new runs and notifies you. Nothing continues in the background and you are never charged overages. Add more credits or upgrade your plan to resume.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my credit package?">
  Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or change your monthly credit amount at any time from your billing settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
</Accordion>

## How to control your spend

Alfera gives you three levers to keep costs predictable:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your monthly package">
    Pick a Pro plan tier from 5,000 credits up to 3,000,000 credits self-serve. Enterprise plans support custom volumes beyond that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use approval gates">
    Any customer-facing or destructive action waits for your one-tap approval before it runs. This prevents accidental over-spend on large jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor live usage">
    View your current balance, credits used this month, and a forecast of remaining spend in real time from your billing dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Approval gates are enabled by default. You can adjust what requires approval in your workspace settings.
</Tip>
